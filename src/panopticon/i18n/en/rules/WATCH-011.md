# WATCH-011 — Comparison withheld

## Problem
The declaration is absent, partial, or not authoritative for the observed tool.

## Impact
Observed behavior cannot be compared exhaustively with claimed scope.

## Evidence
Declaration authority and coverage fields identify the missing comparison boundary.

## Recommended action
Publish a tool-specific authoritative completeness assertion and explicit scope.

## How to verify
Load the declaration again and confirm authority and coverage are complete.

## Limits
This rule reports withheld judgment; it does not turn missing information into a pass.
